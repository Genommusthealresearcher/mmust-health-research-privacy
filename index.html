Privacy Policy

MMUST Health Research

Effective date: 6 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, we may collect names, email addresses, student registration numbers, courses, departments, year of study, profile pictures, posts, comments and messages.

2. Why We Collect Information

We use information to create accounts, authenticate users, verify student eligibility, manage profiles, support academic discussions, protect the platform and respond to support requests.

3. Storage and Security

Application information may be stored using Supabase database and authentication services. We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the platform or disclosed when legally required.

5. User Content

Posts, comments and profile information may be visible to other authorized users. Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information in discussions.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as long as necessary. Users may request account deletion and removal of personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations.

7. User Rights

Users may request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

The platform is intended for eligible university students and is not designed for children.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy as the platform develops. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or account deletion requests, contact the platform administrator through the app's support channel.

MMUST Health Research is an independent student-focused project and is not an official university application.
